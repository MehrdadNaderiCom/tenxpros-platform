This is the complete record of everything built, verified, and deployed in this cycle: the involvement-based redefinition of the partner functions with the seat-count Strong test, the per-function evidence flow, the already-ours defenses, the B2B-only engine enforcement, the richer admin review, the stated decision SLA, the full notification set, and, shipped in the same deploy, the owner's previously approved Academy clarity layer, the partner engagement telemetry, the audio voice fix, and the recaptured registration screenshot. Everything was built additively: no column, config key, or enum value was dropped; every commercial number renders from the config source of truth; no dashes appear anywhere; and the Academy exams kept their question counts with exactly one correct answer per question, answerable from the taught text, never a mutable number.

## 1. The rules now live, in plain language

The dividing line between the partner functions is the partner's level of involvement.

- Basic Introduction (5 percent, both sides). The partner actively introduces and explains us to a person or organisation they genuinely know, and then steps away: zero meetings and no follow-up. The partner-facing copy says plainly that a genuinely valuable introduction is confirmed and rewarded even though the partner introduces and steps aside. It requires the warm-relationship attestation (the one piece of evidence that blocks submission of that claim, mirroring the engine's pay gate), pays only when the introduced contact becomes a closed, paid deal, and confers no account ownership or protection.
- Origination (Qualified 10 percent B2C and 8 percent B2B; Strong 15 percent B2C and 12 percent B2B). Origination goes beyond an introduction: the partner attends the meetings and takes on the follow-up. Qualified versus Strong is decided by SEAT COUNT, counted on PAID AND COLLECTED seats only (pending seats do not count; refunded and cancelled never count), with an INCLUSIVE threshold: B2C Strong is 15 seats or more, decided by seats alone, because an individual has no domain (this fits an individual with a real community who sells volume, like a blogger or influencer); B2B Strong requires BOTH a genuinely New or Dormant company domain AND 10 seats or more. Below the threshold, a new-company B2B deal keeps its new-company classification (the Growth Bonus credit and the override opener anchor survive) but is paid the Qualified rate. A deal with no paid-collected seats yet is always Qualified, never Strong: the conservative rule, never grant Strong on missing data. The old dollar thresholds remain in config, retired.
- Closing (5 percent B2C, 10 percent B2B). The partner drives the deal to a signed, started contract themselves, taking at most one online meeting of under one hour from our side, and carries everything else through payment cleared, contract signed, and engagement started. Payable only with a recorded signed date.
- Delivery or Coaching (8 percent, single company-set rate). Unchanged: normally the company's own; paid when the company engages a partner to help it scale, with a recorded delivered date; fixed fee only as a superadmin exception with a logged reason.
- Renewal Override (50 percent of the opener rate). B2B ONLY, now enforced in the engine (a B2C override line is refused), credited to the account opener while the opener holds Active Status, one line inside the renewal's cap.
- Growth Bonus (1 percent). B2B ONLY, now enforced in the engine (a B2C growth line is refused), counted by distinct genuinely new B2B organisations by canonical domain in the rolling window.
- Caps unchanged and airtight: 25 percent B2C, 30 percent B2B, 35 percent Tier 3 focus, across every function and every partner, with the weighted-split conservation and no-reinflation guarantees intact and re-proven in the deployed container.

## 2. The claim and evidence flow

At registration the partner picks the functions they intend (Basic Introduction is now claimable alongside Origination, Closing, and Delivery) and each checked function opens a small evidence block with the boundary stated in encouraging copy: who the contact is, the relationship, and how they introduced us (with the required attestation) for an introduction; the meetings and follow-up taken on for an origination; how they drive to signature for a closing; the scope for delivery. Every evidence field is optional (the copy says it materially affects confirmation and speeds review); only the attestation blocks a Basic Introduction claim. Claims are stored structurally (one DealFunctionClaim row per function), resubmission prefills them so evidence is never wiped, and the pay path sources the attestation from the claim so the admin never re-asks.

## 3. The defenses and the decision

- Hard blocks at confirmation: the registration is blocked when its NORMALIZED entity name (case, punctuation, and legal suffixes folded) or its canonical domain matches a House Account or another partner's live account. "Acme, Inc." with a fresh domain can no longer slip past "Acme Incorporated".
- Near-match warnings: the review card flags similar names (small edit distance or containment) and related domains as "possible existing relationship," soft only.
- The Panel classification decision: objective by default; the admin may only force existing-company treatment (Qualified rate, no new-company credit) with a required logged reason, notified to the owner. It is consulted through the linked account AND by canonical domain, so recording a deal without linking the account cannot bypass it. No path grants Strong by hand.
- One origination per deal: a second unweighted origination line is refused (weighted-split siblings excepted), so seats collected after the close are handled by reverse-and-re-add, which reclassifies from the current paid seats, never by a silent double pay.

## 4. The admin review, the SLA, and the notifications

The review card now shows everything a real decision needs: the partner and tier, the entity with near-match warnings, the domain with its LIVE newness (New, Dormant, Existing), the estimated seats against the applicable threshold with the likely classification spelled out (and the note that the binding classification happens on paid-collected seats at commission time), every claimed function with its evidence and attestation state, the case, the thread, the decision due date, and the classification decision control. The decision window (5 business days, config-sourced, resolved per partner) is stated on the registration form, in the acknowledgement email, in the terms, and in the Academy. The notification set is complete: the partner is acknowledged on submit and resubmit, told on decline (with the reason), on a recorded closed deal, and on a paid commission; the owner is notified on submit, on close, and on any classification override. All sends are failure-safe and never break the action.

## 6. Adversarial findings in this cycle, all caught and fixed

The refute-first multi-lens review over the whole redesign confirmed six findings; every one was fixed and re-verified:

1. MAJOR, override bypass: the forced-EXISTING classification was consulted only through the linked account, so recording the deal without selecting the account bypassed the Panel decision and paid Strong. Fixed: consulted by canonical domain as well.
2. MAJOR, stale or double origination: seats collected after the close either froze the partner at the Qualified rate forever or allowed a second full-rate origination line. Fixed: the one-origination guard (weighted splits excepted) with the reverse-and-re-add reclassification path.
3. MAJOR, per-partner overrides broken: five config keys (the two new seat thresholds, the single delivery rate, and the two retired dollar thresholds) had no PartnerConfig columns, so saving any per-partner override crashed and per-partner seat thresholds silently fell back to global (partially pre-existing). Fixed: the five nullable columns added to PartnerConfig and the migration.
4. minor, claims wipe: a crafted duplicate function in the submission could violate the unique key mid-replace and destroy stored claims including the attestation. Fixed: dedupe plus skipDuplicates.
5. minor, SLA source: the form stated the decision window from compile-time defaults while the email and admin queue used the resolved config. Fixed: the form takes the resolved value from the server page.
6. minor, stale text: labels and doc comments still presenting the domain-plus-dollar rule as current. Fixed everywhere; the re-verify's own grep confirms no surface or comment presents the dollar test as current.

The re-verification confirmed fixes 1 through 5 held on the first pass and caught two residual stale comments from fix 6, which were corrected; the final grep check is clean. Deterministic gates after every step: tsc clean, content dash lint clean, 390 vitest tests passing (30 files), the production build complete, and the content dump validating with question counts intact.
